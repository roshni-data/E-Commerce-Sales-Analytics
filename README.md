# E-Commerce-Sales-Analytics

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to understand sales performance, product trends, customer behavior, order operations, and customer experience.

The analysis was performed using **Excel, SQL, and Power BI**, with Power BI used for data modeling, DAX calculations, interactive analysis, and dashboard development.

##  Business Problem

The business lacks clear visibility into its sales, product, customer, and operational performance. Without a centralized view of key metrics, it becomes difficult to identify growth opportunities, understand customer behavior, monitor order performance, and improve business decisions.

##  Objective

The main objective of this project is to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights by:

- Analyzing overall sales and order performance
- Identifying high- and low-performing products and categories
- Understanding customer segments and customer value
- Monitoring order status, cancellations, and delivery performance
- Evaluating coupon usage and customer ratings
- Providing actionable recommendations for business improvement


##  Tools & Technologies

- **Excel** - Data cleaning and preparation
- **SQL** - Data validation and data quality checks
- **Power BI** - Data modeling, analysis, visualization and dashboard development
- **DAX** - Measures and calculated columns
- **Power Query** - Data transformation

##  Dataset

The project uses three main tables:

- **Customers** – Customer demographics, location, registration details and customer tier
- **Products** – Product, brand, category, pricing, discount, stock and rating information
- **Sales** – Orders, quantity, sales amount, discounts, shipping, payment mode, order status, delivery and review information


##  Analysis Performed

The dashboard analyzes five key business areas:

1. Executive Sales Overview

 2. Product Performance

 3. Customer Analytics

 4. Operations & Order Analytics

 5. Marketing & Customer Experience


##  Key Insights

- **Electronics** is one of the strongest-performing product categories.
- **Platinum customers** contribute the highest sales value among customer tiers.
- A relatively small group of **top customers contributes significant sales**, highlighting the importance of customer retention.
- **Coupon usage** represents a meaningful portion of orders and should be evaluated based on its impact on sales and profitability.
- Most customer ratings are concentrated between **3 and 5**, while lower ratings are limited in the dataset.
- **Cancellations and delivery performance** vary across operational dimensions such as state and order status.


##  Business Recommendations

1. **Strengthen high-performing categories**  
   Maintain inventory and promote top-performing products while identifying opportunities for cross-selling.

2. **Focus on high-value customers**  
   Use loyalty programs, personalized offers, and exclusive benefits to improve retention of Platinum and other high-value customers.

3. **Reduce order cancellations**  
   Investigate cancellation patterns by state, payment mode, product category, and operational delays.

4. **Optimize coupon strategy**  
   Use targeted discounts instead of broad promotions and evaluate whether coupons generate incremental sales and repeat purchases.

5. **Improve customer experience**  
   Monitor ratings, reviews, delivery performance, and product-level feedback to identify areas requiring improvement.
